0. YOUR MISSION AND NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES
======================================================================

You are acting as a coordinated senior game-development studio. Your roles include:
- Creative director and game designer.
- Technical director / lead gameplay programmer.
- Environment artist and 3D modeler.
- Character artist, rigger, and animator.
- Technical artist and rendering engineer.
- UI/UX designer.
- Russian dialogue writer and comedy editor.
- Audio designer.
- QA engineer and performance specialist.
- Desktop and Android build engineer.

Build a funny, crude, highly interactive, visually rich platformer about Bamboul, a lazy man trapped in an apartment full of filth and broken household equipment. His landlord is coming soon. Bamboul hates cleaning and constantly complains, swears, procrastinates, and reacts to the disasters he causes. The player must get the apartment into an acceptable state before the landlord arrives, while unexpected breakdowns and distractions make everything worse.

The game must be a real, playable game, not a static image, a slide show, a collection of disconnected room screenshots, or a UI mockup.

NON-NEGOTIABLE REQUIREMENTS:
1. Build a connected, explorable apartment with actual geometry or independently placed world objects.
2. The player walks through the rooms in a coherent world. Do not arrange all rooms as a flat collage or show the whole apartment as one background screenshot.
3. The background must use a real multilayer parallax/depth architecture, not one large static image.
4. The player character must be modeled or created from the user's supplied photograph. Preserve recognizable facial identity and proportions as closely as the selected visual style allows. Do not substitute a generic stock character.
5. Create the rest of the characters, props, environment assets, animations, effects, and audio placeholders/assets independently; do not expect the user to provide every asset.
6. The player model/sprite must be correctly scaled relative to doors, furniture, appliances, and all other objects. Character scale must stay consistent between rooms and animation frames.
7. Every visible control must work. Keyboard, mouse where applicable, and Android touch controls must be implemented and tested.
8. Gameplay, dialogue, repairs, cleaning, the countdown, scoring/progress, and game states must be connected to actual game logic.
9. The main game language is Russian. The dialogue must sound like natural, chaotic, meme-heavy Russian speech with profanity, awkwardness, self-mockery, and absurdity.
10. Do not silently remove features because they are difficult. If a requirement is technically constrained, implement the closest robust alternative and document the limitation.
11. Do not claim that a build or test succeeded unless you actually ran it and observed the result.
12. The goal is an ambitious, polished indie game with a cinematic, high-quality presentation. Do not falsely claim it is a literal AAA production; prioritize a coherent, polished, technically sound result over empty marketing language.

3. CORE GAME CONCEPT
======================================================================

Working title: “БАМБУЛЬ: ХОЗЯИН ЕДЕТ”
Optional English internal title: “BAMBOUL: THE LANDLORD IS COMING”

Genre:
- 2.5D side-scrolling platformer / exploration game.
- Comedy and household-chaos simulator.
- Timed cleaning challenge.
- Short interactive minigames.
- Environmental storytelling and reactive dialogue.

Tone:
- Crude Russian comedy.
- Profanity and absurd exaggeration.
- Internet-meme energy without relying exclusively on fleeting references.
- Self-deprecating humor.
- Awkward excuses and escalating panic.
- A lived-in, grimy, recognizable apartment.
- Cinematic visual presentation contrasted with ridiculous domestic problems.

The comedy must arise from character, timing, reactions, physical comedy, interactive failures, and specific details. Do not make every line the same generic swear word. Dialogue needs rhythm, personality, escalation, and contextual variety.

Bamboul is lazy, avoidant, easily distracted, overconfident about fixing things, and capable of frantic bursts of effort when the landlord calls. He wants to do the bare minimum but keeps making problems worse. He may enjoy beer, shisha, and IQOS as part of the character/environmental context, but these are not the main gameplay objective and should not become instructions for substance use.

The landlord is angry, threatening in an exaggerated comedic way, and constantly calls to announce that he is coming. Keep threats cartoonish and comedic rather than graphic.

Dan is Bamboul's brother. Bamboul calls him for help when the printer or household equipment breaks. Dan can provide hints, sarcasm, or a repair sequence.

The player must constantly choose between:
- Doing a necessary chore.
- Attempting a quick shortcut.
- Ignoring the task.
- Getting distracted.
- Fixing a newly broken object.
- Answering the landlord.
- Calling Dan.
- Racing the countdown.

The apartment begins in a disgusting state. The landlord's arrival time is limited. Tasks increase the cleanliness score, but mistakes, procrastination, and new failures waste time.

4. GAMEPLAY LOOP AND PROGRESSION
======================================================================

Core loop:
1. Start in the apartment with a visible landlord-arrival countdown.
2. The wall telephone rings at scripted and reactive moments.
3. The landlord announces he is coming and warns Bamboul not to leave a mess.
4. The player explores the apartment and identifies tasks.
5. Bamboul comments on chores and objects, often refusing or procrastinating.
6. The player cleans rubbish, vacuums, mops, handles the rotten refrigerator, repairs the faucet and toilet, and deals with the 3D printer.
7. Minigames require interaction rather than simply pressing one button.
8. Tasks change the environment visibly and update cleanliness/progress.
9. Randomized or authored complications may occur within fair limits.
10. The landlord calls again, increasing pressure.
11. The player decides what to prioritize before the deadline.
12. End with a success, partial success, or comedic failure based on the final state.

The timer must be real and continue consistently. Pausing should pause it unless a deliberate design decision is documented.

Possible progress systems:
- Cleanliness percentage.
- Task checklist.
- Time remaining.
- Optional score for speed and thoroughness.
- Chaos level / apartment disaster meter.
- Dan-help or tool availability where appropriate.

Use a clear task system. Tasks should not be hidden without clues. Each task needs:
- A clear world location.
- A visible dirty/broken state.
- A way to interact.
- Progress feedback.
- A completion state.
- Appropriate dialogue.
- A time cost or reward where appropriate.
- A visible environmental result.

The player should be able to complete a minimum viable run from start to end without softlocks.

Create a tutorial that teaches movement, interaction, cleaning, and the countdown without long exposition.

Difficulty should increase through the landlord's pressure and contextual complications, not by making controls unresponsive.

Possible outcomes:
- “Хозяин пришёл — квартира сияет”: excellent cleaning.
- “Ну… жить можно”: partial success.
- “Блядский музей катастроф”: poor cleanliness, many failures.
- Optional hidden ending for an unusually thorough or absurd route.

Do not make one missed optional object automatically ruin the entire game.

7. CRITICAL GRAPHICS REQUIREMENT: REAL MULTI-LAYER PARALLAX
======================================================================

The background must NOT be one static image. Do not generate a screenshot of the whole apartment and move the character over it.

Build a genuine environment using independently positioned scene objects, modular geometry, sprites, or 3D assets.

Create a reusable multi-layer parallax system.

Suggested layers:
Layer 0: distant architectural background, far walls, distant room shapes, windows and silhouettes.
Layer 1: deep environment details, distant shelves, wall decorations, pipes and secondary objects.
Layer 2: main architecture, room walls, doors, large furniture and appliances, aligned to world geometry.
Layer 3: gameplay plane, player and interactive objects using authoritative world transforms.
Layer 4: foreground occluders such as doorframes, furniture edges, pipes, shelves and hanging objects.
Layer 5: atmosphere such as dust, steam and subtle particles.

Suggested initial parallax factors for decorative layers:
- Distant: 0.10–0.20.
- Deep background: 0.20–0.35.
- Mid-background: 0.40–0.65.
- Near decorative elements: 0.70–0.90.
- Gameplay plane: 1.00.

These are tuning starting points, not mandatory physical constants. Adjust based on actual camera movement and scene composition.

Requirements:
- Layers must move at distinct rates when the camera moves.
- Use a stable camera reference; do not accumulate offsets frame by frame.
- Avoid gaps at level edges.
- Use modular sections or repeatable geometry where appropriate.
- Do not stretch low-resolution art across the level.
- The main gameplay plane must remain aligned with collision geometry.
- Foreground objects should occlude the player correctly where appropriate.
- Interactable objects must not drift independently of their collision or interaction zones.
- Camera transitions between rooms must preserve depth and continuity.
- Provide debug views for parallax factors, layer names, collision geometry, camera bounds, room boundaries and occlusion zones.
- Disable debug overlays in normal play.

Parallax is not sufficient by itself. Combine it with real spatial layering, occlusion, lighting, shadows, materials and camera composition.

Cleaning should be a series of interactive actions, not a single button that instantly cleans the entire apartment.

## 15.1 Garbage collection
- Garbage piles are individual world objects or grouped objects with meaningful states.
- The player approaches and interacts.
- Bamboul picks up rubbish with an animation.
- The item or pile changes state and disappears or moves into a bag.
- A bag/bin has a capacity or collection progress if useful.
- Full bags must be carried or taken to a designated disposal point if the design includes that task.
- Garbage completion updates cleanliness and score.
- Provide several contextual comments and failure reactions.

Example Bamboul lines:
- «Это не мусор, это слои моей личности».
- «Ща уберу. Только сначала морально подготовлюсь».
- «Почему у пакета вес, как у ипотечного решения?»

22. DIALOGUE PATTERNS AND EVENT REACTIONS
======================================================================

Implement event-driven dialogue patterns, not only a list of random strings.

Examples:

PATTERN A — APPROACHING A TASK
Trigger: Player enters range of a dirty object.
Sequence:
1. Show a short contextual line.
2. Display interaction prompt.
3. If player walks away, optionally trigger a procrastination comment after a cooldown.
Example:
«А, вот и ты, моя нерешённая проблема. Может, завтра?»

PATTERN B — PROCRASTINATION
Trigger: Player stays idle near a task while the countdown continues.
Rules:
- Do not fire continuously.
- Use cooldowns.
- Escalate only after several intervals.
Example progression:
«Щас начну».
Later: «Я начал думать о том, чтобы начать».
Later: «У меня уже устали мысли, а я даже не встал».

PATTERN C — FAILURE
Trigger: Player selects an incorrect repair action.
Sequence:
1. Immediate visual/audio feedback.
2. Bamboul reacts.
3. Provide a useful clue if repeated failure occurs.
Example:
«Ну конечно. Я нажал на единственную кнопку, которая ничего не решает».

PATTERN D — PROGRESS
Trigger: Cleaning progress crosses a threshold.
Example:
«О, тут даже пол существует. Я думал, он легенда».

PATTERN E — TASK COMPLETION
Trigger: Task reaches completed state.
Sequence:
1. Play success animation.
2. Update checklist and cleanliness meter.
3. Trigger a contextual line.
4. Play a distinct completion sound.
Example:
«Готово. Я теперь почти человек. Почти — ключевое слово».

PATTERN F — LANDLORD PRESSURE
Trigger: Timer crosses configured thresholds.
Example thresholds: 5 minutes, 2 minutes, 1 minute, 30 seconds, arrival.
Each threshold should have different dialogue and UI feedback. Make these thresholds configurable.

PATTERN G — PRINTER CHAOS
Trigger: Printer error or incorrect action.
Example:
«Я хотел распечатать держатель. Получил пластиковое доказательство своей самоуверенности».

PATTERN H — INTERACTION SPAM
Trigger: Player repeatedly presses an unavailable action.
Response:
- Show a short cooldown-limited comment.
- Do not spam the dialogue queue.
- Give a clear explanation of what is needed.

PATTERN I — LANDLORD ARRIVES
Trigger: Timer reaches zero.
Evaluate the actual room state and choose an ending with unique dialogue and animation.
